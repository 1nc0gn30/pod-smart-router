Frequently Asked Questions

How does the license key unlock work?

Upon completing checkout via Stripe or DePay Solana, your browser receives an activation parameter (?licensed=true) or you can paste your receipt license key directly into the activation modal. Your key is stored client-side in localStorage, instantly unlocking unlimited dispatch generation and batch payload exports.

Can I run this offline or on a local server?

Yes. POD Smart Router is 100% zero-dependency Python standard library. You can run python3 -m pod_smart_router.server or execute commands via the pod-smart-router CLI directly from your terminal or CI/CD pipelines.

How do I report an issue?

Open an issue on GitHub at github.com/1nc0gn30 or email support@nullai.tech.
